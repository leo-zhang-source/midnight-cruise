Midnight Cruise — 1986

A synthwave highway chase. Start the drive, then weave through slower traffic on a three-lane highway into the city while the police follow: press the left and right arrow keys (or tap the left or right side of the screen) to change lane. The car drives flat out by itself; hold Space or Brake to slow down. Pick up nitro canisters and press T or Nitro to smash through traffic. Driving into the back of a vehicle ends the run; clipping one puts the police on your tail, and a second mistake while they are there gets you busted. A cassette deck controls the music and the colours.
