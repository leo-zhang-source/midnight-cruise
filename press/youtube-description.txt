Neon City. 1986. Make every charge count.

Drive the PHANTOM through a neon-lit police pursuit in MIDNIGHT CRUISE — 1986, a free browser arcade driving game with an original synthwave soundtrack.

Read the traffic. Collect energy. Save your nitro for the moment the road closes in—or turn a blockade into a smash chain. Hit vehicles fly, then dissolve into cyan and magenta digital fragments as the points stack up.

Nitro becomes harder to charge as the threat level rises. Every run gives you just three VHS tapes to rewind your last mistake, restoring the life state you had three seconds earlier. How far can you go, and what grade will you earn?
