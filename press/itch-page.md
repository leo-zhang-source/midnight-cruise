# itch.io page — MIDNIGHT CRUISE — 1986

## Title

MIDNIGHT CRUISE — 1986

## Short description

A synthwave police chase. Three lanes, no brakes, and a VHS rewind when it all goes wrong.

## Description

### Three lanes. No brakes. One more chance.

**Midnight, November 8, 1986. The Neon City Beltway.**<br>
The city glows ahead. Sirens close in behind. You're at the wheel of the PHANTOM, and lifting off isn't an option.

**MIDNIGHT CRUISE — 1986** is a synthwave arcade police chase. Thread the traffic, smash through roadblocks with nitro, and push your score as a lone cruiser becomes a coordinated five-star pursuit. When the run ends, a spare VHS tape can give you back the last three seconds.

**Free to play in your browser. No installation.**<br>
Single player · Keyboard, mouse or touch · Game interface in English

### Make the next gap.

The car accelerates automatically. You choose the lane and the moment to commit. Taxis cut across the road. Cruisers close the gap. AIR-7's searchlight sweeps ahead. Read the signals, slip through the opening, and turn a string of close calls into a higher score.

![The PHANTOM dodges a helicopter searchlight between a police cruiser and a pickup.](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvNTM3NDc0NS8zMjMzNzYzNC5qcGc=/original/EjvfvS.jpg)

### Save the nitro. Or make an entrance.

A nitro burst pushes you towards double speed and lets you smash vehicles out of your path. It also shakes off the pursuit car and restores a lost life. Spend a canister to build your score, or keep it for the moment you're one hit from the end. Carry up to three. Choose your moment.

The longer you last, the harder they hunt: rolling roadblocks, rear-wheel grapplers, flanking interceptors, and helicopter fire sweeping across the lanes. Start at **LV 0** to learn the chase, or turn the starting threat level up to **LV 20** for a faster, more demanding opening.

![A nitro burst carries the PHANTOM through a five-star pursuit.](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvNTM3NDc0NS8zMjMzNzYzMC5qcGc=/original/xS0E1v.jpg)

### The tape still has three seconds left.

Wrecked or busted? Spend a spare tape to **rewind three seconds**. The road rolls back, traffic runs in reverse, and the gap you missed is there again. Every run starts with one tape. Collect 100 energy cells to earn another, up to three in reserve.

### A night caught on VHS.

A cinematic opening drops you into a world of chrome, wireframe mountains and neon skylines. Scanlines and tape distortion frame an original synthwave score, synthesised live and growing more urgent as the chase escalates. Choose **Sunset, Blue Hour, Afterglow or Lagoon**, or let the colours change as you drive.

*Turn the sound on. Headphones recommended.*

### Take the wheel

Click **Run game** above, then **START DRIVE**. The car handles acceleration; there is no brake.

- **Change lane:** ← / → or A / D · click or tap the left / right half of the road.
- **Nitro:** ↑ · NITRO button, when you have a canister.
- **Pause / resume:** P · pause button / RESUME.
- **Settings:** O · gear icon. Adjust music, colours and starting difficulty.
- **Skip the opening:** S · SKIP INTRO.
- **Rewind after a wreck or capture:** R · REWIND 3 S, with a spare tape.
- **Mute / unmute:** M · sound control in Settings.

You start with **two lives**. A hit costs one; another hit before you recover ends the run. Nitro restores the lost life. Your best score is saved in this browser when local storage is available.

Use a current browser with **WebGL 2** enabled. On a phone, landscape gives you more room to read the road. No sound? Click inside the game, then check its volume and mute controls and your browser tab's sound setting.

### 三条车道，没有刹车。把最后三秒，再跑一次。

1986 年 11 月 8 日，午夜。霓虹城环城公路。前方是发光的天际线，身后是越来越近的警笛。你驾驶着 PHANTOM 飞驰，而这辆车，没有刹车。

**MIDNIGHT CRUISE — 1986** 是一款免费浏览器街机追逐游戏。在车流之间找出空隙，赶在探照灯变红前换道，让一次次超车与擦身而过连成更高的分数。坚持得越久，追捕越凶：滚动路障、后轮抓捕装置、侧翼拦截车与 AIR-7 将逐步加入五星围堵。

**留一罐氮气，还是现在突围？**<br>
氮气让你向双倍速度冲刺，撞开沿途车辆，并恢复失去的一条生命。用它冲分，还是留作脱险的后手，由你决定。最多可携带三罐。

**失手了，让磁带倒转。**<br>
撞毁或被捕后，消耗一盘备用磁带，倒回最后三秒，再试一次刚刚错过的路线。每局自带一盘；每收集 100 个能量单元再获得一盘，最多储存三盘。

电影式开场、镀铬车身、霓虹天际线与 VHS 失真，共同构成这场八十年代公路追逐。实时合成的原创配乐随追捕升级而加紧节奏，四种色调可自由切换。打开声音，让警笛与引擎一起进入这个夜晚。

- **换道：**← / → 或 A / D；触屏点击道路左半边或右半边。

- **暂停 / 设置：**P / O，或点击暂停 / 齿轮图标。
- **跳过开场 / 静音：**S / M。
- **结束后倒带：**R 或 REWIND 3 S，需要备用磁带。

每局有两条生命，氮气可恢复失去的生命。支持键盘、鼠标与触屏；手机建议横屏。需要支持 WebGL 2 的现代浏览器。**游戏界面为英文**，最高分保存在当前浏览器，不跨设备同步。[阅读完整中文介绍与操作说明](https://github.com/leo-zhang-source/midnight-cruise/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md)。
