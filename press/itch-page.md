## Description

### Neon City, 1986.

It's just after midnight on the Neon City Beltway. You're driving the PHANTOM, traffic is still moving, and the patrol behind you has called for backup.

**MIDNIGHT CRUISE — 1986** is an arcade driving game about finding a way through an increasingly crowded pursuit. Read the traffic, time your lane changes and keep the run going to beat your best score, accompanied by an original synthwave soundtrack.

**Free to play in your browser.**<br>

### Through the traffic

The car accelerates automatically, so every decision is about where to go next. Turn signals, brake lights and attack warnings give you a moment to react. A taxi may merge into your lane; a cruiser may try to box you in. As the pursuit builds towards five stars, interceptors, rolling roadblocks and AIR-7 join the chase.

![The PHANTOM avoids a helicopter searchlight in the blue night palette.](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvNTM3NDc0NS8zMjMzNzYzNC5qcGc=/original/EjvfvS.jpg)

### A way through the roadblock

Nitro gives you a brief burst towards double speed, enough to smash through vehicles, shake off the pursuit car and recover a lost life. You can carry up to three canisters. Using one early can help build your score; keeping one in reserve gives you an escape when the road closes in.

Start at **LV 0** while you learn the traffic and attack patterns, or choose a starting threat level up to **LV 20** for a faster, more demanding run.

![The PHANTOM accelerates past two trucks on an orange neon highway.](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvNTM3NDc0NS8zMjMzNzkxOC5qcGc=/original/SXxIcm.jpg)

### Rewind the last three seconds

After a wreck or capture, a spare VHS tape lets you return to the moment before it happened. The traffic runs backwards, then the chase resumes with another chance to change lane. Every run starts with one tape. Collect 100 energy cells to earn another, up to three in reserve.

### An eighties night drive

A cinematic opening, wireframe hills and a skyline lit in neon set the scene. Scanlines and tape distortion give the picture the texture of an old recording. The original synthwave score is synthesised live and changes as the pursuit intensifies, alongside the sound of shifting gears and approaching sirens.

Choose **Sunset, Blue Hour, Afterglow or Lagoon**, or let the palette change as you drive. Headphones recommended.

### Take the wheel

Click **Run game** above, then **START DRIVE**. The car handles acceleration; there is no brake.


- **Change lane:** ← / → or A / D · click or tap the left / right half of the road.
- **Nitro:** ↑ · NITRO button, when you have a canister.
- **Pause / resume:** P · pause button / RESUME.
- **Settings:** O · gear icon. Adjust music, colours and starting difficulty.
- **Skip the opening:** S · SKIP INTRO.
- **Rewind after a wreck or capture:** R · REWIND 3 S, with a spare tape.
- **Mute / unmute:** M · sound control in Settings.

You start with **two lives**. A hit costs one; another hit before you recover ends the run. Nitro restores the lost life. Your best score is saved in this browser when local storage is available.

Use a current browser with **WebGL 2** enabled. On a phone, landscape gives you more room to read the road. No sound? Click inside the game, then check its volume and mute controls and your browser tab's sound setting.
