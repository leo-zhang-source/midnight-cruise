## Description

**Midnight. November 8th, 1986. The Neon City Beltway.**
Every cruiser in the county is on your bumper, a helicopter is sweeping the road with its searchlight, and your car has no brake.

**MIDNIGHT CRUISE — 1986** is an arcade police chase in a world of chrome, neon and analog synths. Weave through three lanes of traffic at up to 550 MPH, grab nitro canisters to smash straight through roadblocks, and slip the searchlight before it locks on. The heat climbs every twenty seconds. How long can you stay free?

**Free. Plays instantly in your browser** with keyboard, mouse or touch.

### Features
- **A 26-second cinematic opening.** The chase is on before you touch the wheel.
- **Three lanes, no brakes.** Read the traffic, find the gap, commit.
- **Nitro.** Double speed for 5.6 seconds, and you smash cars, vans and police cruisers out of your way.
- **Police that hunt you.** They throw rolling roadblocks, weaving cruisers, a grappler, an interceptor that cuts you off, and AIR-7's searchlight and fire at you. Every attack leaves a way through: tough, never cheap.
- **Five-star wanted level** and a threat level that never stops rising. Pick your starting level, from a cruise (LV 0) to MOST WANTED (LV 20) at full speed.
- **An original synthwave score**, generated live and locked to the action: nitro hits on the beat.
- **Four colour moods:** Sunset, Blue Hour, Afterglow and Lagoon.
- **VHS rewind.** Crash, rewind the tape three seconds, and take the gap you missed.
